# Actors

## 1. Guest
## 2. Customer
## 3. Admin
## 4. Seller
## 5. Payment Gateway
## 6. Notification Service

--------------------------------

## 1.Guest:
A user who has not yet logged into their account.

### Permissions

- Browse products
- Search products
- View product details
- Contact Support
- View articles (published only)
- View user reviews
- Register
- Login

---------------------------------

## 2.Customer:
Has a user account.

### Permissions

- Browse products
- Search for products
- Add product to cart
- Remove product from cart
- Manage cart
- Place order
- Pay for order
- View order history
- Submit a review
- Manage profile
- Manage addresses
- View product details
- View notifications
- Mark notifications as read
- View articles (published only)
- View user reviews
- Apply coupon code to own unpaid orders
- Remove coupon code from own unpaid orders

----------------------------------

## 3.Admin:
System Administrator.

### Permissions

- User management
- Vendor management
- Management of categories
- Product management
- Management of orders
- Management of reviews
- Management of reports
- Suspension of users
- Manage comments
- Management of articles (create, update, delete, publish, attach products)
- Manage filters
- View product discounts
- Management of coupons
- Management of roles
- Warehouse management
- Dashboard statistics and sales analytics

-----------------------------------

## 4. Seller

A vendor who can manage and sell products on the platform.

### Permissions

- View seller profile
- Update seller profile
- Create products
- Update own products
- Delete own products
- Manage own inventory
- View own orders
- Confirm delivered orders
- View sales reports
- Manage product discounts
- Access seller dashboard

-----------------------------------

## 5.Payment Gateway
External payment system

### Responsibilities

- Verify payment
- Confirm payment
- Reject payment

-----------------------------------

## 6. Notification Service
External SMS and email delivery system.

### Responsibilities

- Send email
- Send SMS
